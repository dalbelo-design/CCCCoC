The gap we're measuring
What official data doesn't capture

HMIS is a contact management system — it records touchpoints with the service system. The Point-in-Time count is a single-night snapshot in January. Together they show the system's activity. Neither shows the experience of the person moving through it.

HMIS misses
  • People not yet in the system
  • What happened after a referral
  • Why someone left or disengaged
  • Barriers at point of service
  • Lived experience of navigation
PIT count misses
  • Doubled-up / couch surfing
  • People in motels
  • Those hiding or avoiding contact
  • Seasonal and weather variation
  • Post-sweep displacement
The diagnostic approach
Lived experience as system testing

Outreach workers on Tuesday and Thursday runs — providing medical care, navigation, showers, food — are already in direct contact with the people the official counts miss. This tool turns those interactions into structured data that maps the gap between what the system thinks it's doing and what people are actually experiencing.

📋
~90 seconds per interaction
7 questions. Worker fills it out on their device during or right after a service interaction. No app to install — works in any browser.
How it connects to the strategic plan
Implementation accountability

Every strategic plan makes assumptions about how the service system works. This tool tests those assumptions in real time — before the next PIT count, before the next HMIS report, before the next grant cycle.

The navigation gap
Between referral and actual service

HMIS records the referral. It rarely captures whether the person made it to the program, whether the program had space, whether the rules allowed them to enter, or whether they gave up before getting there.

Shelter rules as barriers
No pets · No couples · Sobriety requirements · Curfews incompatible with work · Gender-binary facilities · ID requirements
System failures
Program at capacity · Waitlist months long · Phone number disconnected · Staff had nothing to offer · Application process too complex
Trust collapse
Prior negative experience · Immigration fear · Watched others fail · Gave up believing it would work

About this tool

Built for the strategic plan

A service system diagnostic tool for the Contra Costa County Homelessness Strategic Plan — specifically the implementation phase.

The approach
Lived experience as real-time system testing

Every strategic plan makes assumptions about how the service system works. The people moving through that system — right now, this week — are the best possible test of whether those assumptions are correct.

This tool gives outreach workers a simple, fast way to capture what they're seeing during Tuesday and Thursday service runs — and turns those observations into structured data that the coordinating committee and county leadership can act on.

It doesn't replace HMIS. It fills the gaps that HMIS and the Point-in-Time count can't reach — the invisible population, the navigation failures, the barriers at point of service, the people who have stopped trying.

The outreach form — 7 questions
1. Where are you right now? (sub-region)
2. Where did this person sleep last night?
3. Swept or displaced in the past 6 months?
4. Prior contact with shelter or navigation system?
5. If prior contact — what got in the way?
6. What was provided at today's interaction?
7. Worker notes (optional)
Deployment
Tuesday and Thursday outreach runs. Workers use their own devices — no app to install. Form optimized for mobile. Takes approximately 60–90 seconds per interaction.
